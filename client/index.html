The problem

Every launch on Solana
is watched.

Your deployer wallet is funded from somewhere, and that somewhere is public. Bundlers buy in the first block. Snipers land before your community reads the ticker. Copy-traders follow your wallet into every coin you touch.

01

Unlinked launches

Shield SOL into the Vault, claim it into a ghost account, launch from the ghost. The coin shows no wallet, because there isn't one attached.

02

The Fair Window

Every launch opens sealed for 60 seconds. Buys are escrowed, capped at 2 SOL a buyer, then all fill together at one clearing price. Block zero is worth nothing.

03

The guard

For 10 minutes after the window, no holder can pass 2% of supply and every holder waits 3s between trades. Bundles and sniper loops run into a wall.

04

Shielded liquidity

Ghosts trade, launch and withdraw on their own. Withdrawals to a fresh address leave on a randomized delay, so timing doesn't link them back.

05

No mempool to front-run

Trades fill against the curve in the order they arrive, with your own price limit. There's no public pending pool for anyone to sandwich.

06

The Trace scanner

Paste any Solana wallet and see what an onlooker sees: who funded it, who it talks to, how exposed it is. Read straight from Solana.
